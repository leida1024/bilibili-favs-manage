# Design

Keep native confirmation in the DOM transport, with no HTTP or network interception. Observe before a single confirm click. Adapter-owned notification selectors and baseline snapshots reject pre-existing, hidden, unrelated and negative messages. Recognize exact translated operation success texts from public VUI assets. Separate confirmation timeout (30s) from animation delay, and show a still-waiting notice after6s. Explicit failure is distinct from unknown. Preserve FavoriteManager outcome propagation.

Records have two content modes: daily excludes debug, details includes all and technical task/phase identifiers. Retain function scope; explain filtered/evicted counts and unfiltered export. No destructive filter actions.

Both cross-page handoffs capture bounded full inert diagnostics after their existing delay, immediately before navigation; waiting transition precedes capture. Executable tasks stay separate and consumed before side effects. Merge by ID, respect clear tombstones and preserve legacy states. No new replay queue.

Version2.2.3 across package, lock, config and generated userscript. Product/dev records remain separate commits.
